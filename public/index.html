Features

Everything you need to listen

Simple tools that stay out of the way of the music.

Private vault

Upload MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A or OGG files with cover art. Only you can see and play them.

Playlists you can share

Build playlists, add a cover and description, then make them public and share the link.

Likes & history

Like songs to save them, and jump back into anything you played recently.

Queue control

Play next, add to queue, shuffle and repeat — the way a real player should work.

Sleep timer & speed

Fall asleep to music with a timer that fades out, or change playback speed from 0.5× to 2×.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space to play, arrows to seek and change volume, S to shuffle — press ? in the player.
